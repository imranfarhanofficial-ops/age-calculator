How Accurate Age Calculation Works

Our age calculator uses standard calendar boundary logic to account for varying month lengths (28 to 31 days) and solar leap years every 4 years.

Frequently Asked Questions

How are leap year birthdays (Feb 29) handled?

For individuals born on February 29th, in non-leap years, the official age completion is evaluated on March 1st.

Is my personal birth data saved?

No. Calculations are executed strictly inside your browser. No dates are transmitted or stored on any server.
