How Accurate Age Calculation Works

Our age calculator uses standard Gregorian calendar boundary logic to account for varying month lengths (28 to 31 days) and solar leap years occurring every 4 years.

How are leap year birthdays (Feb 29) handled?

For individuals born on February 29th, in non-leap years, the age calculation evaluates their birthday on March 1st.

Is my personal birth date saved?

No. All calculations are executed client-side in your browser JavaScript memory. No data is stored or transmitted.
