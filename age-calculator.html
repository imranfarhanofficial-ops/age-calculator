About Age Calculator

Our Age Calculator helps you find your exact age from your date of birth to any specific date and time.

It provides accurate results in years, months, weeks, days, hours, minutes and seconds.

How to Use

  1. 1 Select your date of birth.
  2. 2 (Optional) Select the date at which you want to calculate age.
  3. 3 Click on the “Calculate Age” button.
  4. 4 Your exact age will be shown in multiple formats.
